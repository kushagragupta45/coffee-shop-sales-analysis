# Coffee-Shop-sales

☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data to uncover key business insights related to revenue, product performance, store performance, and customer purchasing patterns. The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel with Pivot Tables, Charts, and an Interactive Dashboard.

The goal is to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions by identifying sales trends, peak business hours, top-performing products, and store-wise performance.

🎯 Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance.
Identify top-selling products and categories.
Compare revenue across store locations.
Understand customer purchasing behavior by time and day.
Discover peak sales periods for better resource allocation.
Provide actionable insights for improving profitability.
📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains 149,116 transaction records from multiple coffee shop locations.

🔍 Key Insights
Sales Trends
Sales vary significantly across months, indicating seasonal demand patterns.
Certain weekdays generate higher revenue than others.
Product Performance
Coffee products contribute the largest share of sales.
A small number of products generate a significant portion of total revenue.
Store Performance
Some store locations consistently outperform others in both revenue and transaction volume.
Customer Behavior
Peak sales occur during morning and afternoon hours.
Customer traffic decreases during late evening periods.
💡 Business Recommendations
Increase inventory for top-selling products during peak periods.
Launch promotions during low-sales hours to improve footfall.
Replicate successful strategies from high-performing stores.
Introduce combo offers for frequently purchased products.
Optimize staff scheduling based on hourly demand patterns.
